Research

Biodiversity, Community Assembly & Spatial Ecology

I investigate how environmental heterogeneity, spatial structure, dispersal and biotic interactions influence species distributions and community assembly across spatial scales and life stages.

Environmental Change, Species Distributions & Population Dynamics

I study how environmental and climatic change affect species distributions and population dynamics, using spatial modelling, remote sensing and demographic approaches.

Plant Demography, Biotic Interactions & Ecological Modelling

I investigate how local biotic interactions and environmental conditions influence individual plant performance and population dynamics, with an increasing focus on quantitative demographic modelling.

About

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Ecological Modelling at the Helmholtz Centre for Environmental Research (UFZ), Leipzig, Germany.

My research has spanned tropical forests, large-mammal conservation and Arctic ecosystems. Across these systems, I use quantitative approaches to investigate how environmental conditions and spatial ecological processes shape biodiversity and ecological dynamics.
